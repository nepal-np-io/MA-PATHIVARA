The Kanyam Legacy
A Tale of Two Hills

The Spirit of Ma Pathivara

Rising majestically above the morning mist of Suryodaya, Ma Pathivara Holidays is more than a boutique retreat—it is a sanctuary of Himalayan heritage. Every timber used in our architecture and every stone laid in our gardens tells a story of the Ilamic spirit.

Our estate is perched at an altitude that invites the clouds into your morning tea. Here, luxury is redefined through the lens of local authenticity. We offer a piece of the mountain's soul, curated for the discerning traveler.

Divine Flavors

The Legend of Masu Bhat

At the heart of our culinary philosophy is the iconic *Masu Bhat*. This is not merely a meal; it is a celebration of Nepalese hospitality. We source our meat from local hillside farms where animals graze on wild herbs.

Slow-cooked over a traditional wood fire, the spices permeate every fiber of the meat, creating a symphony of flavor that resonates with the heritage of the Himalayas. Served with world-famous Akabare Chillies of Ilam.

Thukpa: Warmth for the Soul

When the evening mist rolls into the estate and the temperature drops, our kitchen prepares the *Thukpa*. This traditional Himalayan noodle soup is our remedy for the cold. Our Thukpa is unique to Ma Pathivara, utilizing hand-pulled noodles and a broth that has been simmered for twelve hours.

Laden with fresh seasonal vegetables harvested from our own organic patch, each bowl provides a nourishing embrace. Whether you prefer it with shredded chicken or as a pure vegetable delight, our Thukpa is the ultimate comfort food for a high-altitude evening.
